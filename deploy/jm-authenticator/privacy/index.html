Privacy & data handling

Privacy Policy

Your authentication codes are generated on your device. This policy explains what JM Authenticator stores, how optional features handle your data, and what stays under your control.

Local code generation

Authentication codes are generated locally. No JM account or registration is required.

No tracking or analytics

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services and does not send your accounts or codes to the developer.

You choose export destinations

JM backups are password-encrypted. Cloud file providers apply their own policies.

01. Scope & publisher

This policy describes how JM Authenticator handles information on Apple devices, including local authentication, account icons, device authentication, backups, imports and transfers. File providers and services you choose have their own privacy policies.

No JM account or registration is required. Authentication codes are generated locally on your device; no network connection is required.

JM Authenticator does not collect or transmit your authentication accounts, secret keys or generated codes to the developer. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, telemetry or crash-reporting services, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics or crash information with app developers, Apple may provide information to the developer under Apple’s own privacy policy. JM Authenticator does not control that setting.

02. Local authentication data

The app processes authentication secrets and associated account information to generate authentication codes on your device. If you create custom account icons, the processed icons are also part of your stored account data.

App preferences and backup-history information are stored locally. Backup passwords are not saved by the app.

03. Secure storage

Authentication secrets, account information and processed custom icons are stored in the secure vault using Apple Keychain protection. Vault items are restricted to this device, are accessible only while the device is unlocked, and are not synchronized through iCloud Keychain.

App preferences and backup-history information are kept in the same protected storage. A small number of non-sensitive display preferences, such as whether the welcome screen is shown, use standard app preferences. Secure storage does not mean that deleting an active account removes every previous snapshot or backup; see Retention & deletion.

04. Camera & photos

You may select existing images or take photographs to create account icons. Processed icons are stored in the secure vault and included in encrypted JM backups.

The camera is also used to scan account setup and transfer QR codes. QR images selected from Photos or Files are processed locally to extract account credentials.

Taking a photo for an account icon creates a temporary image file. The app attempts to clean up this file, but crashes, interruptions or deletion failures may leave temporary files behind. Cleanup is not guaranteed, and temporary files are not guaranteed to be deleted immediately.

Selected original photos are not deleted by the app. Deleting an account or its processed icon does not delete the original photo you selected.

05. App lock, Face ID, Touch ID & privacy protection

You can turn on an optional app lock. When it is enabled, the app asks for Apple’s device authentication — Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode — before showing your accounts. Authentication is performed by iOS. JM Authenticator does not access or store biometric templates.

With app lock enabled, the app can lock again when you return to it and can lock automatically after a period of inactivity that you choose, even while the app remains open.

You can choose to hide authentication codes until you reveal them. When the app becomes inactive or moves to the background, it covers its content so that accounts and codes are not visible in the app switcher.

Google-compatible QR export requires fresh device authentication. The separate single-account setup QR screen relies on the existing app lock without an additional authentication prompt.

06. Encrypted backups

JM backup files are password-encrypted. Backup passwords are not saved by the app. Store your backup password carefully.

When you create a backup, you choose where it is saved:

  • Internal — the encrypted backup is stored locally in the app’s private storage on this device. Internal backups are excluded from iCloud and device backups and are removed when the app is deleted. Because they stay on the same device, they cannot protect you against loss, theft or destruction of that device.
  • External — you save the encrypted backup file to a destination you select, such as a folder in Files or a file provider. JM Authenticator does not control that destination. If you choose a cloud-backed file provider, that provider may upload the file according to its own settings and privacy policy.
  • Both — the app creates an internal copy and lets you save an external copy.

Processed custom account icons are included in encrypted JM backups. Internal backups and backup-history information do not expire automatically.

Backup history is stored locally and may include export time, account count, filename, destination information, a file fingerprint, and a file reference used to reopen the backup when permitted by the file provider. Saving to both internal and external destinations creates separate history records.

07. Importing, restoring & exported QR codes

You can import accounts from Google Authenticator transfer QR codes and from supported 2FAS and Ente Auth export files. Only supported formats can be imported, and files that exceed the app’s size or security limits are declined. Not every export format or version of these apps is supported.

Imports are processed locally on your device. Transfer QR codes are read with the camera or from images you select; import files are read directly from the location you choose in Files. The app does not keep a copy of the selected image or file, and it does not record imported files in backup history. The original file remains where you stored it.

To restore, you select an encrypted JM backup and enter its password. Restoring and importing add accounts that are not yet in the app. Existing accounts are not replaced, and accounts that are already present are skipped as duplicates.

Exported QR codes can contain authentication secrets. Anyone who receives or captures those secrets may be able to generate authentication codes for the corresponding accounts. Treat exported QR codes as sensitive credentials.

QR exports and password-encrypted JM backup files are different transfer formats. The fresh-authentication requirement for Google-compatible QR export and the separate single-account setup QR screen are described under device authentication.

08. Clipboard

When you copy a verification code, it is placed on the system clipboard so that you can paste it. On iOS, the copied code is marked local-only, so it is not shared with your other devices, and is set to expire after at most 30 seconds or when the code expires, whichever comes first.

Locking the app, either manually or through a security lock such as inactivity auto-lock, clears a copied code that is still on the clipboard. Moving the app to the background does not clear the code immediately, so you can paste it into another app; it is removed when it expires. The app clears only a code it copied itself and does not remove content copied by other apps.

Clipboard clearing relies on iOS clipboard behavior and cannot be guaranteed under every system condition. Other applications may retain codes you paste into them, and clipboard expiry does not remove copies already pasted elsewhere.

09. Retention & deletion

Deleting an account removes it from the active account list. Previous encrypted snapshots and backups may retain account information.

Internal encrypted backups and backup-history information do not expire automatically. External exports, original photos and copies retained by other apps or file providers have separate lifecycles.

Deleting the app

Deleting JM Authenticator removes its internal backups and other files stored in the app’s private storage. Depending on iOS behavior, data protected in Apple Keychain may remain on the device after the app is deleted. Do not rely on deleting the app to erase your accounts, and do not rely on reinstalling it to recover them. To keep your accounts, save an encrypted backup to an external destination before deleting the app or changing devices.

Temporary-file cleanup is best-effort. Interruptions, crashes or deletion failures can leave temporary files behind. Deleting an account or a file does not establish comprehensive secure erasure, and this policy does not promise it.

10. Third-party file providers & this website

Cloud-backed file providers may upload exported files according to their own settings and privacy policies. Review the provider and destination you select when saving or sharing an export.

This privacy webpage contains no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, external fonts or third-party content dependencies. When served online, the hosting provider may process request information such as IP addresses and access/error logs. The host’s logging settings and retention are separate from the app’s offline operation.

11. Contact & policy changes

For privacy questions, contact privacy@jegprofil.info. Do not send authentication secrets, backup passwords, recovery codes or credential QR codes with your inquiry.

This policy may be updated when the app’s data handling changes. The effective date above identifies when the published policy takes effect.
