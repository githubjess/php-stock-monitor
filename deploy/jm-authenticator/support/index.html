01. Contact support

For questions, problems or feedback about JM Authenticator, email us at:

support@jegprofil.info

To help us assist you, please include:

  • your device model, such as iPhone or iPad, and its iOS or iPadOS version;
  • the JM Authenticator version, shown in Settings;
  • a short description of the problem and the steps that lead to it.

Never send authentication secrets, setup keys, verification codes, backup files, backup passwords, recovery codes or QR codes that contain account credentials. We do not need them to help you, and JM Authenticator cannot recover a forgotten backup password.

02. Getting started

Step-by-step instructions: User Guide — Add an account.

JM Authenticator generates time-based one-time passwords (TOTP), the six- to eight-digit verification codes that many websites and apps use for two-factor authentication.

Adding an account

  • Scan a QR code — when a service shows a two-factor setup QR code, choose Scan QR code and point your camera at it. Scanning stays on your device.
  • Choose from Photos — select a screenshot or photo of a setup QR code.
  • Choose from Files — select a saved QR code image (PNG, JPEG or HEIC, up to 8 MB).
  • Enter a setup key — if the service offers a text key instead, enter the service name, account name and key manually.

Standard otpauth TOTP setup codes are supported. Counter-based (HOTP) codes are not supported.

Using verification codes

Each code is valid for a short period, usually 30 seconds, and a countdown shows when the next code appears. Copy the code, then paste it into the service that asks for it. A copied code is cleared from the clipboard within 30 seconds or when the code expires.

If a code is rejected

Codes are calculated from your device clock. If a service rejects valid-looking codes, check that your device’s date, time and time zone are set automatically and are correct.

Keep each service’s own recovery codes in a safe place, separately from JM Authenticator.

03. Backup & restore

Step-by-step instructions: User Guide — Create a backup and Restore a backup.

JM Authenticator does not provide a cloud backup. To protect your accounts against loss of your device, create encrypted backups in Settings → Encrypted backups.

Backup types

External backup (recommended)
Saves an encrypted .jmbackup file to a location you choose, such as a folder in Files or iCloud Drive. External backups are easy to transfer and restore on another device.
Internal backup
Saves the encrypted backup privately inside JM Authenticator on this device. It is intended for local recovery, cannot protect you against loss of the device, and is removed when the app is deleted.
Both
Creates an internal copy and lets you save an external copy at the same time.

Backup passwords

Every backup is protected by a password of at least 12 characters that you choose. Use a long, unique password. You need the same password to restore your accounts later.

Keep your backup file and its password safe. Backup passwords are not saved by the app, and a forgotten backup password cannot be recovered — not by JM Authenticator and not by support.

Restoring a backup

Select a backup from the backup history or choose a backup file, then enter its password. Before anything is imported, the app shows how many accounts are in the backup and how many are new. Restoring adds accounts that are not yet in the app; existing accounts are kept, and exact duplicates are skipped.

Before deleting the app or changing devices, save an external backup and confirm that you still know its password.

04. Import & export

Step-by-step instructions: User Guide — Import accounts.

You can move accounts into and out of JM Authenticator in Settings → Import & Export Accounts. All processing happens locally on your device. Importing only adds accounts you select; existing accounts and backups remain unchanged, and duplicates are skipped.

Importing

Google Authenticator
In Google Authenticator, use Transfer accounts → Export accounts, then scan every QR code shown, or select screenshots of them. Google Authenticator migration formats version 1 and 2 are supported.
2FAS Authenticator
Choose a .2fas backup file and enter its password if it is protected. 2FAS backup schema versions 3 and 4 are supported.
Ente Auth
Choose an Ente Auth export in encrypted (recommended) or plain-text format. Plain HTML exports are not supported.

Only standard TOTP accounts can be imported. HOTP, Steam and other unsupported account types are skipped, and the app explains which entries cannot be imported. Because other apps can change their export formats, not every version of their exports may be supported.

QR screenshots contain your authentication secrets. After you have verified the import, delete such screenshots from Photos, including Recently Deleted.

Exporting

  • Export as QR codes — shows Google Authenticator–compatible transfer QR codes after you confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Only 30-second TOTP accounts with 6 or 8 digits can be exported this way, and JM labels and icons are not transferred. Whether another app accepts these codes depends on that app.
  • Export to encrypted file — saves the selected accounts to a password-protected file that you can later restore in JM Authenticator.

Anyone who scans exported QR codes may gain access to those accounts. Treat them as sensitive credentials.

05. Icon Studio

Icon Studio lets you give each account a recognizable icon. Open an account’s edit screen and choose Change Icon. A live preview shows how the icon will appear.

Built-in icons

Choose from a library of symbols and service logos, and select a background colour from the preset colours or a custom colour adjusted by hue, saturation and brightness. You can restore the default icon at any time.

Personal images

You can also use your own picture from the Photo Library, the camera or Files. Images up to 8 MB and 24 megapixels are supported.

  • Crop — pinch to zoom, drag to position and rotate the image.
  • Customize — adjust size and alignment, select the image background with an adjustable tolerance, and change its colour or make it transparent. You can undo changes or restore the original image.

Processed icons are stored securely with your accounts and included in encrypted JM backups. Your original photos are not changed or deleted.

06. Security & privacy

Security options are in Settings → Security & app lock.

App Lock
Requires Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode before your accounts are shown. Authentication is performed by iOS; JM Authenticator does not access or store biometric data.
Lock when returning to the app
Requires authentication again after you leave JM Authenticator.
Auto-lock after inactivity
Locks the app after a period of inactivity that you choose, even while it remains open.
Hide authentication codes by default
Keeps codes hidden until you tap to reveal them. Countdown timers remain visible.

When the app moves to the background, its content is covered so that your accounts and codes are not visible in the app switcher.

If Face ID or Touch ID changes or becomes unavailable, use your device passcode. There is no bypass or remote reset, so keep your device passcode and an external backup available.

JM Authenticator contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send your accounts or codes to the developer. For full details, read the JM Authenticator Privacy Policy.
