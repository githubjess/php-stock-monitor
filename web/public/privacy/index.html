02. Hosting & server logs

This website is hosted by one.com. When you open a page, your browser sends technical request data to the web server, including your IP address and the page or file requested. The hosting provider processes this data to deliver the website and to keep it secure and stable, and may record it in server logs.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use this data to identify visitors or to create usage profiles. How long server logs are kept is determined by the hosting provider.

03. Contact form

When you use the contact form, we process the name, email address, subject and message you enter. They are sent by email to business@jegprofil.info, with your email address set as the reply address, so that we can answer your enquiry. You can also write to that address directly.

The legal basis is the handling of your enquiry: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where it relates to a contract or steps before one, and otherwise our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any follow-up, unless statutory retention obligations require longer storage.

Protection against misuse

To prevent automated misuse, the server limits how many messages can be sent within 15 minutes. For this, it stores the time of each submission together with a keyed hash of your IP address, not the IP address itself. Entries older than 15 minutes are discarded the next time the limit is checked. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in protecting the form against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Please do not send passwords, authentication codes or account secrets through the form.

04. Share Monitor

Share Monitor shows market information for the ticker symbols you choose. Your browser requests this information from our server. Our server then retrieves the market data from Yahoo Finance; these server requests contain the ticker symbols and currency pairs, not your IP address or other information about you.

Your tickers and Share Monitor settings are stored in your browser, as described under Browser storage. The legal basis for processing the requests is our legitimate interest in providing the function you use (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

05. Browser storage

This website uses no cookies. It stores the following information in your browser’s local storage:

  • Website language — only after you choose a language in the language menu (jm-authenticator:website-language).
  • Share Monitor — your tickers, related listings, signal settings and display currency (stock-monitor:tickers, stock-monitor:related-listings, stock-monitor:signal-settings, stock-monitor:panel-currency).

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us, except that Share Monitor sends your ticker symbols to our server to retrieve market data. It is stored because it is necessary to provide the functions you request, so no consent is asked for (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time by clearing this website’s data in your browser.

06. Recipients

  • one.com — hosts this website and processes the request data described above.
  • Email delivery — contact form messages are delivered by email to the mailbox business@jegprofil.info.
  • Yahoo Finance — receives our server’s requests for market data, which contain no personal data about you.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. There is no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

07. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access your personal data (Art. 15), to rectification (Art. 16), to erasure (Art. 17), to restriction of processing (Art. 18) and to data portability (Art. 20).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where processing is based on our legitimate interests, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

To exercise your rights, contact privacy@jegprofil.info. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the authority in the EU member state where you live or work. For the controller of this website, the competent authority is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA).

08. Changes

This notice is updated when the website’s data processing changes. The effective date above shows the current version.
