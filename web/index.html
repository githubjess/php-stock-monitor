Features

Your accounts, under your control.

Offline TOTP

Generate secure codes without an internet connection.

Encrypted Backup

Keep your accounts safe with encrypted backup and restore.

Easy Transfer

Import from supported authenticator apps, including Google Authenticator, 2FAS and Ente Auth. Export using Google-compatible QR codes or encrypted JM backups.

Protect access with Face ID or Touch ID, optional app locking, and privacy controls.

Find your accounts quickly with favorites, search, and flexible list and gallery views.

Use JM Authenticator in English, German, Spanish, French, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese.
